Tips for good samples

  • Only one face should be visible in the frame.
  • Capture samples under a couple of different lighting conditions if possible.
  • Slightly vary head angle between captures (straight, slight left/right turn).
  • Keep a normal distance from the camera — not too close, not too far.
  • Avoid heavy motion blur; hold still for each capture.

Aiming for {{ samples_target }} samples gives the recognizer enough variation to be reasonably tolerant of everyday changes in pose and lighting. When you're done, go to the Students page and click Retrain Recognizer.
